# Static Training program
	- ## Hold as long as possible with proper form.
	- ## Superiors
		- Active Hangs - high bar.
		- Active Static Push Up - up position
		- Hold Arms to the side thumb down - arms parallel to the ground
		- Active Hand Stand supported on the wall or bench.
	- ## Core
		- Hollow body abs hold
		- Superman hold
		- Transversus abdominal hold - pilates breathing technique.
		- V-seat hold with glutes on the ground.
	- ## Inferiors
		- Squat hold with back at the wall
		- Squat hold without support
		- Standing Front leg static raise - Hip flexor
		- Bend Over static hold - posterior leg chain
- # Daily Static Holds Routine (8 weeks)
	- **Principle**: One max-time hold per exercise. Rest 45–90s between holds.
	  Order rationale: Alternate pushing/pulling, upper/lower/core to avoid localized fatigue.
		- ## Warm-Up (5–7 min)
		- Arm circles + band pull-aparts (or shoulder mobility drills)
		- Cat–cow + thoracic extension
		- Hip openers (dynamic leg swings, deep squat prying)
		- 1–2 light plank/hollow activations (~10s each)
	- ## Main Static Holds
		- Upper body pull → **Active Hang* (bar)
		  (primes shoulders, grip, scapular control)
		- Lower body push → **Wall Squat Hold**
		  (safe quad activation, joint-friendly intro)
		- Core anterior → **Hollow Body Hold**
		  (deep ab activation, neutral spine)
		- Upper body push → **Static Push-Up Hold** (top position)
		  (locks scapular stability, chest/shoulder endurance)
		- Posterior chain core → **Superman Hold**
		  (balances hollow body, low back + glutes)
		- Lower body posterior → **Bend Over Static Hold**
		  (hamstrings + glutes, light stretch & stability)
		- Shoulder endurance → **Arms to Side Hold, thumbs down**
		  (lateral deltoid + posture stabilizers)
		- Core static + hip flexors → **V-Seat Hold**
		  (abs + hip flexors, avoids redundancy with hollow by spacing)
		- Lower body unsupported → **Freestanding Squat Hold**
		  (more dynamic stabilization, ankles/knees active)
		- Upper body vertical push → **Handstand Hold** (wall-supported)
		  (finisher for shoulders + CNS, avoid early fatigue)
		- Breathing & deep core → **Transversus Abdominis Hold** (Pilates breathing)
		  (done last for mindful finish, reinforces posture + recovery)
	- ## Cooldown (5 min)
		- Child’s pose + shoulder stretch
		- Deep lunge hip stretch
		- Hamstring stretch
		- 2–3 deep diaphragmatic breaths in lying position
	- ## ⚖️ Notes on structure
	- By alternating upper → lower → core, you’ll reduce overlap and keep intensity high for each hold.
	- Handstand is placed near the end, since it’s neurologically demanding — this way your pressing muscles are pre-fatigued but safe.
	- Breathing/TVA activation at the end doubles as a reset and prevents finishing “tense.”

		- ### 🔧 Adaptação neuromuscular à corrida
		- ### Ajuste recomendado (primeiras 3 semanas):
		- #### 🏃 Corrida (3x/semana)
		  
		  **Sessão A – Leve técnica**
		- 20–25 min bem leve
		- Cadência solta, passada curta
		  
		  **Sessão B – Intervalado curto**
		- 8 × 200 m
		- Ritmo forte
		- Recuperação: 1 min andando
		  
		  **Sessão C – Ritmo controlado**
		- 10 min leve
		- 8–10 min em ritmo de prova
		- 5 min leve
		  
		  👉 Distâncias curtas para **evitar lesão** e “acordar” a musculatura.
